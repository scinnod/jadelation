This translation service uses the technology of DeepL (deepl.com) via a professional *DeepL API Pro* subscription to provide you with high-quality machine translations.

**Your benefits at a glance:**

- **High translation quality** – DeepL delivers natural-sounding results in numerous languages.
- **Data protection** – Unlike the free offerings on deepl.com, your texts are not permanently stored via the API Pro and are not used to train AI models. This means you can also translate confidential or personal content.
- **Convenient and always available** – Translate texts easily via this web interface without needing your own DeepL account.

This service is provided to you through a centrally funded subscription.
