This translation service uses the technology of DeepL (deepl.com) via a professional *DeepL API Pro* subscription to provide all members of Jade University of Applied Sciences with high-quality machine translations.

**Your benefits at a glance:**

- **High translation quality** – DeepL delivers natural-sounding results in numerous languages.
- **Data protection** – Unlike the free offerings on deepl.com, your texts are not permanently stored via the API Pro and are not used to train AI models. This means you can also translate confidential or personal content.
- **Convenient and always available** – Translate texts easily via this web interface without needing your own DeepL account. The service is available to you after login, even from outside the university network.
- **Glossary support** – University-specific terms and preferred translations are automatically applied through centrally managed glossaries.

This service is provided to you by Jade University of Applied Sciences through a centrally funded subscription.
